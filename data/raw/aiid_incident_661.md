Title: Leonardo AI's Platform Alleged to Have Been Used for Creating
Nonconsensual Celebrity Deepfakes
Reported dates: March 26, 2024

Description: Leonardo AI, a Sydney-based startup, faced allegations that its
text-to-image generation platform was exploited to produce nonconsensual
sexual imagery of public figures. Users on Telegram and Reddit reportedly
shared prompts that circumvented the system's content moderation safeguards
to create these deepfake images. The report, from Emanuel Maiberg at 404
Media, indicated that the text-to-image generator developed by Sydney
startup Leonardo AI has been used to create deepfake pornography of
celebrities.

Developer: Leonardo AI (Sydney-based startup)
AI system: Text-to-image generator
Users involved: Telegram community members, Reddit users, and Leonardo AI
platform users
