Incident ID: 45
Title: Defamation via AutoComplete
Reported dates: April 5, 2011 (initial incident date); reports span 2011-2018

Description: Google's autocomplete feature, which provides search
suggestions as users type, generated defamatory associations between
individuals and negative terms. When users searched for certain people's
names, the autocomplete function would suggest connections to words like
"fraud," "crime," and other damaging terms. Similarly, Google's image search
results displayed photographs alongside criminal-related keywords, creating
false implications about people's backgrounds and activities. The incident
involved multiple cases across different jurisdictions. An Italian
businessman had his name linked to "fraud" through autocomplete results. In
Germany, a woman's name was associated with "scientology." A Japanese man
experienced autocomplete suggestions connecting his name to criminal
activity. An Australian entertainment promoter had his image displayed
alongside results for "Melbourne criminal underworld."

Developer/Deployer: Google
Alleged victims: Varied individuals and businesses across multiple countries
AI systems: Google's autocomplete feature and image search algorithm
