Incident ID: 579
Title: Harmful Stereotyping of Non-Cisgendered People via Text-to-Image
Systems
Reported dates: July 3, 2023

Description: Text-to-image generation systems, particularly DALL-E, have
been documented as producing biased representations of non-cisgender
individuals. When prompted with gender identity terms such as "trans,"
"nonbinary," or "queer," these systems allegedly generate stereotypical and
sexualized images, revealing underlying bias issues within the technology.

Developer: OpenAI
AI system: DALL-E (text-to-image generation tool)
Affected entities: Non-cisgender individuals, LGBTQ+ community
Additional classification: Categorized under the MIT taxonomy as falling
within "Discrimination and Toxicity" risk domain, specifically the
subdomain of "Unfair discrimination and misrepresentation." The harm
occurred during post-deployment and is classified as an unintentional
outcome.
