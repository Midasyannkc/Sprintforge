Incident ID: 248
Title: Reported Automated License Plate Reader Alert on Previously Stolen
Rental Car Leads to Alleged Wrongful Detainment in California
Reported dates: November 23, 2018

Description: A privacy advocate named Brian Hofer was detained at gunpoint
after an automated license plate reader flagged his rental vehicle as
stolen, despite the car having already been recovered. During the traffic
stop on Interstate 80 near Oakland, California, officers conducted what was
characterized as a high-risk stop and held occupants at gunpoint before
determining they were innocent. Hofer subsequently filed a federal lawsuit
alleging excessive force and claiming database errors contributed to the
incident. The incident exemplifies failures in AI system robustness
occurring post-deployment, classified under the MIT taxonomy as an
unintentional safety limitation.

Developer: Vigilant Solutions
AI system: Vigilant Solutions Automated License Plate Reader (ALPR)
Deploying agency: Contra Costa County Sheriff
System type: Automated license plate readers (ALPR) and AI-enabled decision
support systems
