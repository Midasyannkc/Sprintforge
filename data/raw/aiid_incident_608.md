Incident ID: 608
Title: UnitedHealth Accused of Deploying Allegedly Flawed AI to Deny Medical
Coverage
Reported dates: February 28, 2023 (incident date); November 2023 onward
(lawsuit filing and media coverage)

Description: UnitedHealthcare, the largest health insurer in the United
States, faced a class action lawsuit alleging systematic misuse of
artificial intelligence to deny healthcare coverage. The company deployed an
algorithm with a "90% error rate" to override physician recommendations for
post-acute care, including nursing home and rehabilitation facility stays.
This resulted in premature patient discharge and substantial out-of-pocket
costs for elderly Medicare Advantage beneficiaries. The lawsuit claims the
algorithm systematically denied medically necessary care despite
contradicting clinical judgments, disproportionately harming elderly
patients who required extended rehabilitation services. The Centers for
Medicare & Medicaid Services subsequently clarified in February 2024 that
insurers cannot use algorithms to determine care or deny coverage on
Medicare Advantage plans.

AI system: nH Predict algorithm (developed by NaviHealth, a UnitedHealth
subsidiary)
Classification: Enterprise AI systems and AI-enabled decision support
systems
