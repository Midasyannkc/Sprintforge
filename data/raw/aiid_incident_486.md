Incident ID: 486
Title: AI Video-Making Tool Abused to Deploy Pro-China News
Reported dates: December 1, 2022

Description: A pro-China influence operation called Spamouflage misused
Synthesia's AI video generation platform to create propaganda content. The
campaign produced videos featuring artificially generated news anchors
delivering pro-China messaging. A news anchor with perfectly combed dark hair
and a stubbly beard outlined what he saw as the United States' shameful lack
of action against gun violence, while other synthetic anchors promoted
Chinese geopolitical interests. The operation disseminated these deepfake
videos across social media platforms including YouTube, Facebook, and X
(Twitter) under channels like "Wolf News."

AI system: Synthesia's AI-generated video-making tool
Developer: Synthesia (a British company)
Malicious actor: Spamouflage Dragon (pro-China influence operation)
Affected entities: YouTube users, Facebook users, X (Twitter) users, and
Synthesia itself
Classification: "Malicious Actors & Misuse" in the MIT taxonomy, specifically
disinformation and influence at scale occurring in the post-deployment phase
with intentional harmful intent.
