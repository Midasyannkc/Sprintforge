Incident ID: 1
Title: Google's YouTube Kids App Presents Inappropriate Content
Reported dates: May 19, 2015 (incident occurrence); reports span 2015-2018

Description: YouTube's content filtering and recommendation algorithms exposed
children to disturbing and inappropriate videos on the YouTube Kids app. The
platform's systems failed to adequately screen or restrict access to content
featuring profanity, sexual material, substance references, and graphic
imagery despite the app being designed for users under 13. Problematic videos
included off-brand cartoons with disturbing scenarios. YouTube's "up next" and
recommendation features facilitated exposure to inappropriate material. The
incident remained unresolved across multiple years, with disturbing content
continuing to surface through 2018. 14 total reports documented the incident.

Developer/Deployer: YouTube (Google)
AI systems involved: Content filtering algorithms, recommendation algorithms,
human moderation systems
