Incident ID: 146
Title: Research Prototype AI, Delphi, Reportedly Gave Racially Biased Answers
on Ethics
Reported dates: October 22, 2021

Description: The Allen Institute for AI created Ask Delphi, a research model
trained on Reddit discussion threads designed to provide ethical guidance on
moral dilemmas. Upon public deployment, the system demonstrated problematic
racial bias in its responses. The incident revealed significant limitations
in language models trained on crowdsourced moral judgments, as the AI would
generate discriminatory advice when presented with scenarios involving
different racial groups. This was meant to be technology designed to make
moral judgments, yet instead exhibited harmful biases reflecting problematic
patterns in its training data.

AI system: Ask Delphi (research prototype)
Developer: Allen Institute for AI
Affected group: Minority groups
Classification context: The incident was categorized under discrimination
and toxicity risks, classified as unintentional post-deployment harm
occurring during the AI's operational phase.
