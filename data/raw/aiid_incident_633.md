Incident ID: 633
Title: Nine Network's AI Alters Lawmaker Georgie Purcell's Image
Inappropriately
Reported dates: January 28, 2024

Description: Nine Network Melbourne broadcasted an altered image of
Victorian state parliamentarian Georgie Purcell during a news segment about
duck hunting legislation. The photograph was modified to display her in
more revealing attire, with enlarged breasts and an exposed midriff, when
she was actually wearing a full dress. Nine Network initially attributed the
changes to automated functionality within Adobe Photoshop's Generative
Expand tool, claiming it was used to resize the image. However, Adobe
subsequently contradicted this explanation, stating that such modifications
would have required deliberate human intervention rather than occurring
through automation alone. The incident generated significant public
backlash regarding sexism in media and the responsible deployment of
AI-powered editing tools.

AI system: Adobe Photoshop's Generative Expand tool
Developer: Adobe
Deploying organization: Nine Network (Australian television broadcaster)
Harm classification: Unfair discrimination and misrepresentation;
unintentional post-deployment harm
