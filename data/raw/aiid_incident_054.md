Incident ID: 54
Title: Predictive Policing Biases of PredPol
Reported dates: November 18, 2015

Description: Predictive policing algorithms designed to assist law
enforcement in forecasting future criminal activity demonstrated patterns of
biased outcomes. The system showed signs of disproportionately targeting
low-income neighborhoods and communities of color, particularly Black and
Latino residents. Between 2018 and 2021, the software directed police patrols
in areas affecting more than one in 33 U.S. residents. The incident
highlights how crime statistics used as training data perpetuated existing
enforcement patterns and systemic biases, resulting in algorithmic
amplification of discriminatory policing practices rather than objective
crime prediction.

AI system: PredPol (the predictive policing software)
Developers/Deployers: PredPol (developer and deployer), Oakland Police
Department (deployer)
Affected parties: Oakland residents
